About us

The Talawa Truck

Likkle but talawa. Small, but mighty.

We’re Talawa Caribbean Food — one little truck serving Jamaican food in Stevenage. You’ll find us at the Man in the Moon every Saturday.

See the menu Find us

Our food

One tray.No corners cut.

Boneless curry goat. Jerk chicken. Rice & peas, mac & cheese and plantain. Our menu brings the mains and sides together in one proper tray.

Everything is cooked fresh on the day and served as people arrive. We’re here on Saturdays, 4–8pm or until we sell out.
