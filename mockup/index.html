Before you set off

CommonQuestions

When are you open?

Every Saturday, 4pm until 8pm — or until we sell out, whichever comes first. We are not open any other day.

Where do I find you?

In the car park at the Man in the Moon, Broadwater Cres, Broadwater, Stevenage SG2 8ET. Look for the trailer with the Jamaican flags on the counter.

Do you do catering or parties?

No. We are Saturdays at the Man in the Moon and nothing else — it keeps the food consistent and the queue moving.

I’ve got an allergy — what should I do?

Tell us when you place your order and we will talk it through with you. Please inform us of any allergies or dietary requirements before you order — our food is prepared in a small kitchen where allergens are present.

Can I order ahead?

No — everything is cooked fresh on the day and served in the order people arrive. Come down and join the queue.

Do you take card?

Both. Card and cash are fine.

Is there anything vegetarian?

Not at the moment. The board is built around the jerk chicken and the curry goat, so there is no vegetarian main. If that changes it will be on the board first.

What time do you usually sell out?

Answer needed

A rough honest answer — “the goat usually goes by 6” — gets people there earlier.

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking in the Man in the Moon car park, which is where you will find the trailer.

Saturday reminder

Never MissA Saturday

The truck is out four hours a week. Most people who mean to come don’t decide against it — they just forget which Saturday it is.

So: one message on a Saturday morning saying we’re on and what’s cooking. That is the whole thing. No offers, no newsletter, nothing on a Tuesday.

Your address is used for the Saturday message and nothing else. It is never sold or passed on, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe. Privacy notice still to be written.

About this mock-up

The full page for Direction 1 — “Likkle But Talawa”: opening screen, tray sequence, menu, gallery, find us, footer. Built for real so you can judge the feel rather than read a description.

Now using your real photos

  • The tray sequence runs on your actual tray shot, with the six labels measured onto it.
  • Your logo is in the top corner and the hero; the sign artwork closes the footer.
  • The truck photo opens the gallery.
  • The tray photo is portrait and shot at an angle under indoor light — it works, but a landscape top-down one in daylight would make this section noticeably better.

Try these

  • Scroll the tray section slowly — it only reads properly at scrolling speed.
  • Drag the gallery sideways. The panels lean as it moves.
  • Click through the three “How to order” tabs in Find Us. You said you hadn’t decided — this is that decision made visible. Whichever you pick, the rest of the page doesn’t move.
  • Open it on your phone: the tray labels become a list, and a Call / Directions bar sticks to the bottom of the screen.

What’s standing in

  • The tray is drawn, not photographed. Your photos came through as chat attachments, so I could see them but not use the files. The drawing sits at the exact position and proportions of your real shot, and the labels share its coordinate grid — the photo drops in behind them without moving anything.
  • The gallery is the photo brief. Every panel is a shot waiting to be taken. Two are drawn in so you can see the sizing.
  • Prices are blank and anything in orange is unconfirmed. I’m not publishing a number or an address I’ve guessed at.
  • The map is drawn rather than a real map — a proper one needs your postcode.
  • The logo isn’t here. It goes top-left, and I want the original file, not a screenshot.
  • “See you Saturday” in the footer is my placeholder. Give me a line you actually say and it’ll be better.
