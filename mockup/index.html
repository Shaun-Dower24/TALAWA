About this mock-up

This is the opening screen and the tray sequence from Direction 1 — “Likkle But Talawa”, built for real so you can judge the feel rather than read a description. Scroll back up and take it slowly — the tray sequence only reads properly at scrolling speed.

What’s real

  • The typography, palette, spacing and motion — all final-quality.
  • The label choreography: each slab and its hand-drawn leader line draws in on its own beat, then everything clears for the closing line.
  • The gold ticker band speeds up and slows down with how fast you scroll.
  • Palm fronds drift with the mouse on desktop.

What’s standing in

  • The tray is drawn, not photographed. Your photos came through as chat attachments, so I could see them but not use the files. The drawing sits at the exact position and proportions of your real tray shot, so swapping it in is one line of code — the six labels and leader lines already point at the right spots.
  • The Talawa logo isn’t here yet — the real one goes top-left, and I’d want the original file rather than a screenshot.
  • Your town is missing from the strapline until you confirm it.
  • On a phone the labels become a simple list under the tray — leader lines pointing across a small screen don’t work.
